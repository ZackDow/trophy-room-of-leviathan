## Ground rules

- **No assessed university work.** No assignments, exam answers or graded code.
- **OverTheWire:** progress and lessons only. No passwords, no full solutions.
- **picoCTF:** writeups only for past competitions and the practice gym.
- **Course honour codes come first.** If a course asks students not to publish solutions, I describe what I built instead.
- **No personal details** such as student ID, email or location in any file or image.
- **Honest history.** Commits land when the work is done. No padded or backdated commits; gaps during exams and holidays stay visible.
- **Light images.** Compressed, metadata stripped, and only when a written summary isn't enough.

Commit style and the pre-commit checklist are in [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md).
